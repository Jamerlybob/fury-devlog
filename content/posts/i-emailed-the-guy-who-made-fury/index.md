## Where I left off

I'd decided to try reviving *Fury*, a PvP MMO that Auran shut down in 2008. I
had the client, a plan, and a nagging question I couldn't shake: **am I actually
allowed to do this?**

Not in a "will I get in trouble" panic way. More that this game was somebody's
work. A studio full of people built it, poured years into it. It felt wrong to start pulling it apart and rebuilding it in
public without at least asking the people who made it how they felt about that.

So before I wrote a line of anything, I tried to ask.

## What I tried

I went looking for whoever was in charge back then. Auran became N3V Games, which
still exists (they do the *Trainz* series now), and the co-founder, Tony Hilliam,
was still around and reachable. I found a work email and wrote to him directly.

I kept it honest and short. Who I am (a hobbyist in NZ, trying to move into
software as a career). What I've got (a full client install from just before the
shutdown). What I'm doing (reverse-engineering the game's packages to understand
how it worked). And what I actually wanted from him: his blessing for a personal,
non-commercial revival, and any thoughts he felt like sharing.

Then I waited, and figured the realistic outcomes were "no reply" or a polite
"please don't."

## What he said back

Four days later:

> We have no issues with you doing anything you can with the game. 
>
> I assume you're using AI to help you? (If not, you should).
>
> Feel free to share your progress and if it looks interesting I may be able to
> convince some old staff members to dig through the archives for any dusty old
> DVDs sitting in a cupboard.

I read that a few times. "We have no issues with you doing anything you can with
the game" is about as clear as it gets.

## What this means

A few things, and I want to be straight about all of them.

**I have the founder's blessing.** The person who ran the studio that made *Fury*
knows what I'm doing and is fine with it. That was the thing I was actually
nervous about, and it's now a yes in writing. 

**And the founder told me to use AI.** "I assume you're using AI to help you? (If
not, you should)." Yep. I definitely am.

**DVDs** I'll return to that. Could be promo material on the DVDs, could be the entire server stuff that I'm missing. I will forge ahead without for now. 

One last thing, for anyone thinking about a project like this. Ask first. It cost
me one carefully-written email and four days of waiting. Worst case is a "no" and
you've saved yourself weeks. Best case is a founder telling you the game was
"best ever and worst ever" and to go for it.
