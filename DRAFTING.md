# Drafting devlog posts

This guide was folded into the project's single human-facing doc to stop the two
copies drifting apart.

**Read it there:** `../docs/FOR-JAMES.md`, section 6 ("How posts should read"),
in the parent `Fury_Project` repo.

It covers: draft vs publish, the front matter, the section skeleton, the voice
blend, images and explainer diagrams, and the non-negotiables (no em dashes,
first person, no invented tidy endings).

The short version, so this file isn't useless on its own:

- Full drafts, not outlines. `draft: true` until James flips it.
- Headings: `Where I left off / What I tried / What broke / What I learned / Next up`.
- Voice: casual, first person, self-deprecating, explain every term to a total
  beginner in one sentence. Show the confusion before the fix.
- Never manufacture a resolution. An honest stuck post is the point.
- Every post carries a picture of any concept it leans on, not just screenshots.
- No em dashes anywhere.
